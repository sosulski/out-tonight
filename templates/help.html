Help

Getting started

  1. Sign up with a username and password.
  2. On your profile, enter your home city, like Chicago, IL.
  3. Open your dashboard to see the weather and events.

Questions

How do I see another city? Type it in the search box on your dashboard.

Why do I only see sports? Your profile's favorite event type filters the list. Choose "Any" to see everything.

Where are my saved events? At the bottom of your profile. Click ★ again to remove one.

"Couldn't find" a city? Use the city and a 2-letter state, with a comma: Austin, TX.
